YouTube on your Nintendo 3DS

A homebrew YouTube client for the 3DS / 2DS family. Search, watch, download and keep your account, all on the console.

Download and install

  1. Scan the QR with FBI (Remote Install > Scan QR code), or copy IkersTube.cia to your SD card and install it.
  2. IkersTube.3dsx also runs from the Homebrew Launcher.
  3. For sound you need the DSP firmware dump at sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc.
  4. The app checks for updates on start (Settings > Update).

Your data lives in sdmc:/3ds/IkersTube/. Test builds are on the Releases page.

Features

Search and watch

Filters, suggestions, Shorts, live streams and chat, subtitles, SponsorBlock.

Your account

Sign in for subscriptions, likes, playlists, history and comments.

Offline

Save videos or audio, queue, favorites and watch later.

Handy extras

Sleep timer, data saver, bookmarks, QR codes for links, themes and backups.

Problems?

If the app closes at start, it enters safe mode on the next launch and resets its settings. Please open an issue and attach crash.txt / log.txt from sdmc:/3ds/IkersTube/.
