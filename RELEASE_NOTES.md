## What's new

- **Watch later.** A new Later tab in the Library and a Later button on the video page. If you are signed in with OAuth, additions and removals are also sent to your YouTube Watch later playlist.
- **Mini player.** A Mini button opens big controls on the bottom screen (previous, play/pause, next, volume, progress). Swipe left for the next video in the queue, swipe right to restart. Press B to close.
- **Quick speed button.** Cycles the playback speed from 50% to 200% without opening the menu.
- **Bookmarks.** Save a moment of a video with a name and jump back to it later (long press a bookmark to delete it).
- **Next chapter button.**
- **Grid of thumbnails.** Optional two-column grid for search results.
- **Start screen.** Choose Home, Library or Search as the screen the app opens on.
- **Automatic theme.** Optional dark theme at night and light theme during the day.
- **Screensaver.** After a minute of pause the logo bounces on a black top screen.
- **Log files.** Warnings and errors are written to sdmc:/3ds/IkersTube/log.txt (the previous session is kept as log_previous.txt). Settings has a button to save the full log.
- **Shared links.** Every QR code you show is also saved to sdmc:/3ds/IkersTube/shared_links.txt and the link is printed under the code, because the 3DS has no clipboard.
- **Translations.** Update messages and older screens are now translated too.
