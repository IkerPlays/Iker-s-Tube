## What's new

- **Smarter updates.** Stable releases are installed automatically when the app starts. Development builds are only offered (red dot on the menu button and the Update button in Settings) and you decide whether to install them. A new Settings switch lets stable installs also see devbuilds.
- **Unique devbuilds.** Every development build is now its own release, versioned `<next version>-dev.<build>`.
- **Continue where you left off.** Reopening a video resumes from the minute you stopped; finished videos start from the beginning.
- **Sleep timer.** Pause playback automatically after up to 120 minutes (Playback tab).
- **Update status in the menu.** The menu shows "Updating..." and "Updated: restart the app" while an update is installed.

## Improvements

- Release notes are shown in plain text inside the app.

## Fixes

- Version comparison is exact: a stable release always wins over the devbuilds of the same version.
