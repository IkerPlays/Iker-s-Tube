## What's new

- **Listen with the console closed.** Audio-only playback now turns the screens off after a few seconds so the battery lasts much longer. A setting controls how fast the screens switch off.
- **Color themes.** Pick Ruby, Ocean, Forest, Violet, Amber or AMOLED black in Settings.
- **Subtitle settings.** Choose the subtitle size (small, normal, large) and color (white, yellow, cyan, green).
- **Automatic quality.** If playback keeps stalling, the next videos start at a lower quality; after a few minutes of smooth playback the quality goes back up.
- **Search suggestions.** The search keyboard now suggests words from your search history and some common terms while you type.
- **Explore shortcuts.** The search screen has ready-made searches: Music, Trending, Gaming, Live, Movie trailers and News.
- **QR code of the video.** Press the QR button on a video page to show a code on the bottom screen and open the video on your phone.
- **Scan a QR code.** From the search screen, scan a QR code with the camera to open a YouTube link.
- **Boot screen.** The app now starts with its logo, and loading texts use an animated ellipsis.
- **Large thumbnails.** Optional bigger thumbnails in video lists.
- **Audio-only screen.** A clean top screen with the title, author, time and a progress bar.
- **Data usage counter.** Settings show how much data this session and the whole install have used.

- **Text size.** A global small / normal / large text size in Settings.
- **Dim while playing.** Optional: the bottom screen gets dark after 20 seconds without touch while a video plays.
- **Network and error info.** Settings show the Wi-Fi state and the last warnings and errors.

- **Player tools.** Buttons to jump 10 seconds back or forward, an A-B loop for repeating a section, and a tappable chapter list taken from the video description.
- **Equalizer presets.** Flat, Bass, Voice and Treble in the equalizer.
- **Seek preview.** While dragging the progress bar you see a thumbnail of the moment you are jumping to (when YouTube provides it).
- **Comments.** Sort comments by top or newest.
- **Discover.** The search screen suggests more from the channels you watched recently.
- **QR sharing from the library.** Long press a video in the queue, favorites or a playlist to show its QR code.
- **Wi-Fi reconnect.** If the connection drops during playback the app asks the console to reconnect.
- **Auto-restart after updates.** When an update is installed as a CIA the app now restarts by itself.
- **Settings in sections.** Options are grouped under clear headings in the right tab.
- **More languages.** The new texts are translated to French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Japanese too.
- **New idle screen.** The spinning cube is gone: the top screen now shows the logo.

## Improvements

- Subtitles now load with more caption formats and retry with explicit formats when YouTube returns an empty answer.
- The update screen shows a short summary of the release notes instead of all of them.
- All new options and screens are available in English and Spanish.

- Shuffle now gives a different order every time you start it.
