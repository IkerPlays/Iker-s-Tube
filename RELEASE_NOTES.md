## What's new

- **Search history.** The empty search screen now lists your recent searches; tap one to search it again, or clear the list.
- **Search filters.** A new button under the search box cycles through filters: all, videos only, today, this week, this month, short (under 4 min) and long (over 20 min) videos. Changing it repeats the current search.

## Improvements

- The search screen has a second row for the filter button; results keep working as before.
