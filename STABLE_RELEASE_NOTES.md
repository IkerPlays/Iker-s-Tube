Iker's Tube is a YouTube client for the Nintendo 3DS family, built on top of FourthTube and ThirdTube (GPLv3).

## Highlights

- **Smooth playback.** A sliding-window buffer keeps the video downloading ahead of you using the SD card, drops what is behind, and retries on network errors instead of freezing.
- **Resilient playback.** If one playback client stops working, the app tries the others automatically.
- **Library.** A play queue you can reorder, favorites, and named playlists with play, shuffle and repeat. Long press a video anywhere to queue it.
- **Smarter search.** Recent searches and filters for type, date and duration.
- **Continue where you left off.** Videos resume from the minute you stopped.
- **Sleep timer, speed and pitch controls, and an audio/video sync adjustment** in the player.
- **Automatic updates.** Stable releases install themselves when the app starts; development builds are only offered, and you decide.
- **New look.** Dark theme with a red accent, animated menu and thumbnails that fade in.

## Installing

Install `IkersTube.cia` with FBI, or run `IkersTube.3dsx` from the Homebrew Launcher.
The DSP firmware dump (`sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc`) is needed for sound.
