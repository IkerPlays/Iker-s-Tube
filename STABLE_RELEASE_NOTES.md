Iker's Tube is a YouTube client for the Nintendo 3DS family, built on top of FourthTube and ThirdTube (GPLv3).

## What's new since 1.0.5

### Changes
- **QR at this time.** New player tool: the QR of the video link starting at the current minute.
- **Seek step.** A setting chooses how many seconds the D-pad left and right jump (5, 10, 15 or 30).
- **Chapter names.** The chapter tools show the name of the chapter they jump to.
- **Queue.** New button removes the repeated videos.
- **Downloads.** The long press menu of a saved video can show its QR code.
- **Info.** The Info tool also lists the subtitle languages and chapters.
- **Time left.** A setting makes the player bar show the time left instead of the length.
- **Battery.** The player warns at 15% and 5% battery.
- **Save playlists.** New player tool saves the playlist you are watching to your YouTube library.
- **Current chapter.** The chapter list highlights the chapter that is playing.
- **Channel page.** Shuffle and Surprise me buttons for the videos of a channel.
- **Videos in a row.** A setting stops the queue and autoplay after 3, 5 or 10 videos (good to fall asleep).
- **Updates.** The update check uses the current repository address directly.
- **About.** Tap the GitHub address to see its QR code.
- **Downloads.** The sort button can also sort by channel.
- **Export.** Export lists also writes your subscriptions as a CSV that other apps can import.
- **Import.** Import subscriptions from a Google Takeout style CSV file.
- **Export.** Every local playlist is exported to its own text file.

## Installing

Install `IkersTube.cia` with FBI, or run `IkersTube.3dsx` from the Homebrew Launcher.
The DSP firmware dump (`sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc`) is needed for sound.
